When to get help now

Any bleeding, or a reduction or change in your baby's movements, means you contact your doctor or hospital straight away, every time. Also go in for severe pain, a high fever, a bad headache with blurred sight, or a fall or blow to your bump. If your mind feels heavy, you can call MANI in Nigeria on 0809 111 6264.

Want private help?

You can book a 1:1 with a licensed practitioner. Your own doctor is always your first call for anything urgent.
