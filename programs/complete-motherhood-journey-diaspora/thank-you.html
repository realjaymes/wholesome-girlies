Your safety net

Each toolkit has its own warning-signs guide. If you ever notice a red flag, at any stage, contact your doctor or clinic straight away. In Nigeria you can reach MANI on 0809 111 6264. In the UK you can call Samaritans on 116 123. In the US or Canada you can call or text 988.

Want private help?

You can book a 1:1 with a licensed practitioner. Your own doctor is always your first call for anything clinical or urgent.
