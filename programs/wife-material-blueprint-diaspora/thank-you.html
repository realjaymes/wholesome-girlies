Your safety first

This is about choosing well, never about tolerating harm. If a partner frightens you, controls you, isolates you, or hurts you, please reach out to someone you trust and a domestic-violence helpline. In Nigeria you can call the Domestic and Sexual Violence Response Team on 0800 033 3333. Your safety comes first.

Want private help?
