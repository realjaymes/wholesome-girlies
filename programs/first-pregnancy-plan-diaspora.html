Everything in your First Pregnancy Plan

Five parts, one keep-forever plan, walking the whole journey: know what's happening → is this normal → what can I eat → understand your scans → get ready for birth.

  • Part 1 — Know What's Happening (Week by Week).
    What is happening to your body and your baby, trimester by trimester and week by week, what to expect and when, plus the free week-by-week tracker and due-date tool.
  • Part 2 — Is This Normal?
    Which symptoms are common at each stage (morning sickness, aches, discharge, swelling) and which need a call, the warning signs, and exactly when to go in. Any bleeding, or any change in your baby's movement, means you contact your doctor or maternity unit straight away, every time.
  • Part 3 — What Can I Eat, and How Do I Feel Human?
    Safe foods in plain terms, including the Nigerian foods you actually eat (jollof, suya, local fish, zobo), the coffee, fish, and spicy-food questions, and a straight word on herbal mixtures, plus safe exercise, weight, sleep, and the body changes no one warns you about.
  • Part 4 — Understand Your Scans & Tests.
    What each scan and test is, why it is done, and the exact questions to take to your doctor or midwife, so you walk in prepared. It helps you understand. It never reads your results for you or replaces your care team.
  • Part 5 — Get Ready for Birth & the First Days.
    Signs of labour and when to go, the hospital bag, your birth plan, what your baby actually needs (and what is a waste of money), and the first-days basics, so you meet labour prepared instead of panicked.
