When to get help

See a doctor after about a year of trying under 35, about six months at 35 or over, or sooner if your periods are irregular or you have a known condition. If the waiting is weighing heavily on your mind, you can call MANI in Nigeria on 0809 111 6264. In the UK you can call Samaritans on 116 123. In the US or Canada you can call or text 988.

Want private help?

You can book a 1:1 with a licensed practitioner. Your own doctor is always your first call for anything clinical.
