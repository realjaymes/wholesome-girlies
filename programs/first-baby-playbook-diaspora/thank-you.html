Want private help?

When you want more than the guides and the community, you can book a 1:1 with a licensed practitioner. Your own doctor is always your first call for anything urgent.

When to get help

Trust your instincts. Get medical help now for trouble breathing, a fit, a high fever in a young baby, a rash that does not fade when pressed, a very floppy or hard-to-wake baby, or choking. This never replaces an infant first-aid course or your doctor. If your own mind feels heavy, you can call MANI in Nigeria on 0809 111 6264.
