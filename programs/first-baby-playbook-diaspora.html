One trustworthy playbook, in five parts

The First Baby Playbook walks you through the early years in five parts: know what's normal, get through the sleepless nights, feed with confidence, read the milestones without the panic, and keep your baby safe and know when to call the doctor. You get the tools to follow it, a resource library that stays yours, and clear answers when you need them, wherever in the world you are raising your child. No conflicting advice, no judgment, no one selling you fear. Just steady guidance that takes you from second-guessing every decision to sure of yourself.

Straight guidance, without the conflicting advice

Every child grows at their own pace, and the milestone charts are a guide, not a race. We help you understand what is typical at each age and what is worth a closer look, so you can filter the noise and trust your own judgment instead of the loudest relative on the call. When something needs real attention, a missed milestone, a feeding or growth worry, or anything that does not sit right with you, we point you straight to your paediatrician. And on the two things that matter most for safety, we do not cut corners: our sleep guidance follows official safe-sleep advice, and we always tell you to learn infant first aid and call for help in an emergency. We explain and support. We never diagnose, treat, or replace your child's doctor.

Everything in your First Baby Playbook

Five parts, one keep-forever playbook, walking the whole journey: is this normal → sleep → feeding → milestones → when something's wrong.

  • Part 1 — Is This Normal?
    The reassurance behind almost every worry: the normal ranges for poop, rashes, crying, weight, and behaviour, what is common at each stage, and what is worth a call.
  • Part 2 — Sleep.
    Why your baby won't sleep, age-appropriate schedules and naps, and gentle ways to help, all built around safe-sleep guidance. It helps you get through the sleepless stretch; it will never promise your baby sleeps through the night.

    Milk and formula, how much and how often, starting solids and first foods, and the fussy-eating stage, without the ten contradictory answers.

    What to expect and roughly when, growth charts explained (a guide, not a race), plus the toddler-years stuff no one warns you about: tantrums, toilet training, speech, and teething.

    Common illnesses and when to see a doctor or go in now, how to soothe a crying baby, and the safety essentials: safe sleep, choking and infant first aid, babyproofing, and vaccinations. It helps you recognise and act. It is never a substitute for a first-aid course or your doctor.

    A private education-only assistant for the small, common questions, day or night and in any time zone, that shares general information, never medical advice, and always points anything urgent to your paediatrician.
