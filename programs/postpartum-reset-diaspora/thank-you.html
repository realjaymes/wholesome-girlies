Your safety net

The When to See a Doctor red-flag guide is inside your toolkit. If you ever notice a warning sign, contact your doctor or clinic straight away. In Nigeria you can call MANI on 0809 111 6264. In the UK you can call Samaritans on 116 123. In the US or Canada you can call or text 988.

Want private help?

You can book a 1:1 with a licensed practitioner. Your own doctor is always your first call for anything urgent.
