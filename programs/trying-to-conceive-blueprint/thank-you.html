Want private help?

When you want more than the guides and the community, you can book a 1:1 with a licensed practitioner. Your own doctor is always your first call for anything clinical.

When to get help

See a doctor after about a year of trying under 35, about six months at 35 or over, or sooner if your periods are irregular or you have a known condition. If the waiting is weighing heavily on your mind, you can call MANI in Nigeria on 0809 111 6264.
