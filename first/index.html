Meet Wholesome Girlies

Women's health you can trust, at every stage.

Wholesome Girlies is a community of women. We are here for you at each stage of your life where you are looking for answers and getting the least support: dating and relationships, trying to conceive, pregnancy, the weeks after birth, and the baby and toddler years.

For each stage, we have our own tools, programs and a community of women living it too, so you're never alone. Our tools help you understand and plan. They are educational, not medical advice, and we tell you when something needs a real doctor.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. You don't pay anything, and you don't need a card. Your first month is free when you claim your place. You can earn up to 11 more free months by using the app.

How do I earn more free months?

Use the app. You earn months when you add the app to your phone, finish your first lesson, join our Telegram group, use the app 3, 7 and 30 days in a row, and tell us what you think on day 7. You also earn months for each friend who joins. You can see the full list above.

What happens when my free months are over?

You can buy your program if you want to keep reading it, but you don't have to. You can still use all our tools, and anything you saved in them stays on your phone.

Can you see what I type into the tools?

No. What you type into a tool stays on your phone and never comes to us. We keep the details you give us when you claim your place: your name, email, WhatsApp number and stage. We also keep your progress, like your streak and which lessons you have read.

Why are places limited?

We are starting with a small group so we can learn from every woman who joins and make the programs better. The number of places on this page is the real number.

Can I share this with a friend?

Yes. Send her your invite link from the app. She gets her own free place. You get 2 extra months for each friend who joins and adds the app to her phone, up to 3 friends.
