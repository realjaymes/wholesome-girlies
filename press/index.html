Our founder

James Praise built Wholesome Girlies with his wife, to help women through the hardest stretches of reproductive life: relationships, fertility, pregnancy and postpartum. He researches the questions women ask at each stage and decides which tools and guides we make next. Read more about James

About Wholesome Girlies

Wholesome Girlies is a health app and community for African women at home and in the diaspora, starting in Nigeria. It gives women the tools, resources and research-backed guidance to close the information gap at each stage, so every woman who uses it learns what to ask, what to watch out for, and when to go to hospital.

What a woman types into a tool stays on her phone, and we never collect it. An account is optional, and she can ask us to delete it at any time.
