Wholesome Girlies vs Flo vs Clue

Last updated: November 2026

Written by Cynthia Obinatu, community and editorial lead, and James Praise, founder and researcher · Every claim comes from a cited source. Not yet reviewed by a clinician, so read it with our medical policy.

For women choosing a period tracker while trying to conceive

Choose Flo if you want daily symptom entries, reminders and a large health library. Choose Clue if you want detailed cycle guides and more than 200 symptoms and experiences to track. Choose Wholesome Girlies if you want a simple fertile day calculator, dates kept on your phone, clinic questions and a plan written for Nigerian women.

Wholesome Girlies is ours, so we are not neutral. We use our own tools and prices for facts about it. We use each app's own pages and Nigerian App Store or Google Play listing for facts about that app.

This page compares pregnancy and cycle apps. It is educational, not medical advice, and no clinician has reviewed it yet.

Where Wholesome Girlies is ahead

✓Your dates stay on your phone

The period dates you enter are saved on your phone. A forget button clears them.

✓Clinic questions for both of you

Print your questions for the fertility doctor. Send your husband a checklist for his sperm health and sperm test questions.

Where Flo and Clue are ahead

Flo has deeper daily tracking

Flo lets you note more than 70 symptoms, sends reminders and has a large health library.

Clue tracks more factors

Clue lets you note more than 200 symptoms and experiences. Its Conceive mode estimates fertile days.

Questions women ask

Does Clue sell health data to advertisers?
Clue's privacy policy says the sensitive health data you track is never shared with or sold to advertisers. It says limited technical and usage data may be shared for advertising when permission is on.
How much is Clue Plus?
The listed price depends on the store. Nigeria shows ₦1,500 (about $1) to ₦12,900 (about $10). The United Kingdom shows £7.99 monthly and up to £24.99 yearly. The United States shows $9.99 monthly and $39.99 yearly.

When to see a doctor

Call your hospital or doctor if you have heavy bleeding, severe pain, trouble breathing, a severe headache, blurred vision, a high fever, or reduced baby movement after movements have started.
