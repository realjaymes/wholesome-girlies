Period Calendar alternatives in Nigeria

Last updated: November 2026

Written by Cynthia Obinatu, community and editorial lead, and James Praise, founder and researcher · Every claim comes from a cited source. Not yet reviewed by a clinician, so read it with our medical policy.

For Nigerian women who want another cycle tracker

Choose Wholesome Girlies if you want to see your fertile days without an account or ads. Choose Flo if you want to note symptoms and get reminders in the app. Choose Clue if you want a tracker with detailed cycle guides. Period Calendar still suits women who want reminders, daily entries and years of history.

Wholesome Girlies is ours, so we are not neutral. We use our own tools and prices for facts about it. We use each app's own pages and Nigerian App Store or Google Play listing for facts about that app.

This page compares pregnancy and cycle apps. It is educational, not medical advice, and no clinician has reviewed it yet.

Wholesome Girlies

Best for fertile days and clinic questions
No app store needed

Choose it if You want your dates kept on your phone and questions you can print for your doctor.

What you can do

  • ✓See fertile days from your last period and cycle length.

  • ✕You cannot note symptoms or moods.

Flo

Best for symptoms and reminders
Number 1 in Nigeria

Choose it if You want to note symptoms each day and see your entries on another phone.

What you can do

  • ✓Note more than 70 symptoms.
  • ✓Get reminders from the app.
  • ✓Use Anonymous Mode without a name or email.

What it cannot do

  • ✕Its articles serve a global audience.

What it costs in Nigeria Android users in Nigeria get Premium at no payment for one year. On iPhone it costs ₦3,900 (about $3) to ₦29,900 (about $22).

Clue

Best for cycle guides
4.4 from 3k ratings

  • ✕The guidance is not written for Nigerian care.

What it costs in Nigeria Clue Plus costs ₦1,500 (about $1) to ₦12,900 (about $10) on the Nigerian App Store.

Why women look elsewhere: what they say about Period Calendar

We sampled 2,583 Period Calendar reviews with one to three stars. Ads appeared most at 17%. App problems came next at 11%, then paying at 6%. Another 56% did not match any topic we counted.

Why women look elsewhere

Period Calendar is widely used and gives women reminders, symptom entries and years of history. Google Play says it contains ads. Some reviewers also mention full-screen ads and trouble moving their entries to a new phone.

What to check

  • Check whether the app shows ads.
  • Check how you move your dates to a new phone.
  • Check whether you can take anything useful to your clinic.

Questions women ask

Which Period Calendar alternative has no ads?
The Wholesome Girlies Period Tracker and Ovulation Calculator show no ads. Flo and Clue have their own payment and feature rules.
Can I use Period Calendar without an account?
Yes. Its App Store listing says you can use it without creating an account. An account or email is used for backup.

When to see a doctor

Call your hospital or doctor if you have heavy bleeding, severe pain, trouble breathing, a severe headache, blurred vision, a high fever, or reduced baby movement after movements have started.
