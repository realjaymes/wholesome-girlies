Flo alternatives in Nigeria

Last updated: November 2026

Written by Cynthia Obinatu, community and editorial lead · Every claim comes from a cited source. Not yet reviewed by a clinician, so read it with our medical policy.

For Nigerian women who want another option beside Flo

If you are trying to conceive and want to see your fertile days without signing up, choose Wholesome Girlies. If you want reminders and years of period dates in one app, choose Period Calendar. If you like reading expert-written guides beside your tracker, choose Clue. If you want one app that stays with you through pregnancy and after the birth, choose Ovia. No app here can tell you the exact day you ovulate from dates alone.

Wholesome Girlies is ours, so we are not neutral. We use our own tools and prices for facts about it. We use each app's own pages and Nigerian App Store or Google Play listing for facts about that app.

This page compares apps. It cannot tell you when you will conceive. A calendar predicts and does not detect. It is educational, not medical advice, and no clinician has reviewed it yet.

Our four picks, ranked

1

Wholesome Girlies

Best if you are trying to conceive and want a plan
No app store needed

Choose it if You want to see your fertile days without signing up, keep your dates on your phone, and go to the clinic with your questions ready.

What you can do

  • ✓Type the day your last period started and see your fertile days on the same screen.
  • ✓Log the first day of each period. After three, it works out your own cycle length.
  • ✓Tap Add to my calendar to put your next periods and fertile days in your phone calendar, with a reminder 2 days before.
  • ✓Print a list of questions for your fertility doctor.
  • ✓Send your husband a checklist of what he can do for his sperm health.
  • ✓Add the Girlies app to your home screen straight from our page, with no app store download. Once it is added, the tools work even when you have no data.
  • ✓Keep using it when you get pregnant, after the birth and while you raise your child.
  • ✓For a full plan, buy The Trying-to-Conceive Blueprint, paid once with no subscription. It explains your cycle, getting your body ready, his part, your tests and the wait, and you join a community of women who are trying too.

What it cannot do

  • ✕You cannot note symptoms or moods.
  • ✕It cannot detect ovulation. It counts 14 days back from your next expected period, so we make no accuracy claim.

What it costs in Nigeria The tools need no card. The Blueprint is one payment of ₦5,700 early-bird (₦13,700 standard), or $12 early-bird ($27 standard) abroad. There is no subscription and nothing to cancel.

2

Clue

Best if you like reading guides beside your tracker
4.4 from 3.1k ratings

Choose it if You want a cycle tracker with expert-written guides, and you do not mind advice that was not written for Nigeria.

What you can do

  • ✓Track your cycle in an app made in Berlin.
  • ✓Read expert-written guides about your cycle.
  • ✓Clue says it does not sell your data to advertisers.

What it cannot do

  • ✕Forecasts of your next dates need Clue Plus, its paid version.
  • ✕Its guides are not written for Nigeria. It ranks 46 in the Nigerian App Store Health and Fitness chart.

What it costs in Nigeria Clue Plus costs ₦1,500 (about $1) to ₦12,900 (about $10) on the Nigerian App Store.

3

Period Calendar

Best for reminders and years of history
4.8 from 11k ratings

Choose it if You want reminders before your period and want to keep years of period dates in one app.

What you can do

  • ✓Get reminders before your period.
  • ✓Keep years of period dates in one calendar.
  • ✓It ranks 2 in the Nigerian App Store Health and Fitness chart and first in Nigerian Google Play search for period tracker.

What it cannot do

  • ✕It shows ads. Google Play marks it as containing ads, and reviews mention ads and upgrade pop-ups.

What it costs in Nigeria Its paid versions cost ₦500 (about $0.40) for 7 days of Premium, up to ₦69,900 (about $52) for Pro, on the Nigerian App Store.

4

Ovia

Best for one app through pregnancy and after the birth
4.5 from 856 ratings

Choose it if You want the same app while you try, while you are pregnant and after your baby comes.

What you can do

  • ✓See a daily fertility score.
  • ✓Switch to pregnancy, postpartum or menopause mode.
  • ✓Read from over 2,000 articles, its listing says.

What it cannot do

  • ✕Some features are paid purchases inside the app, on both stores.

What it costs in Nigeria Google Play Nigeria lists purchases inside the app from ₦4,270 (about $3) to ₦29,000 (about $22).

Questions women ask

Is there a Flo alternative that needs no account?
The Wholesome Girlies Period Tracker and Ovulation Calculator open with no sign-in. Period Calendar's App Store listing says you can use it without an account. Flo's Anonymous Mode lets you use Flo without giving your name or email. Clue offers an account at no cost.
What does Flo cost in Nigeria?
Flo's Pass It On Project gives Premium, its paid version, at no charge for one year on Android in Nigeria. Google Play also lists Premium at no charge in Nigeria. On iPhone, the lowest price is ₦3,900 (about $3). The highest is ₦29,900 (about $22).
Which alternative is best for trying to conceive?
Choose Wholesome Girlies if you want clinic questions and a partner checklist. Choose Clue for expert-written guides. Choose Period Calendar for reminders and years of saved dates.
Do these apps predict ovulation accurately?
No app on this page can detect ovulation from dates alone. A Journal of Medical Internet Research analysis of 7,043 tracked cycles found ovulation does not sit reliably at the midpoint. Take your last three or four period dates to a clinician.

When to see a doctor

See a doctor after twelve months of trying, or six months if you are over 35. See one sooner if your cycles are very irregular, shorter than 21 days or longer than 35, or have stopped. Take your last three or four period dates with you.
