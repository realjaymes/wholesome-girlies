Use Wholesome Girlies for a period and ovulation calendar that opens with no account and no card, keeps your dates only on your phone, and sits beside a paid plan for the wait, questions for the clinic and a checklist for your partner. We do not claim our calculator predicts better than Flo. It is plain arithmetic, and the page shows the sum.

Where Wholesome Girlies is ahead

✓No account and no card

Open the Ovulation Calculator, type the first day of your last period and pick your cycle length. Your fertile window shows on the same screen. There is no sign-in and no payment step.

✓Your dates stay on your phone

Logged period dates sit in storage on your phone. The page says: Saved only on this phone. Nothing is sent to us. A forget button clears them.

✓Your own average, after three periods

Log the first day of each period. After three, the tracker replaces the cycle length you typed with your own average. It uses up to your last six gaps and skips any gap under 18 days or over 60 days as a missed log.

✓New phone, same dates

The Girlies app has Save a backup and Restore a backup. Your saved entries go into one file you keep. Once the app is installed, the tools also work with no data.

✓Written for Nigeria and the diaspora

Prices show in naira for Nigerian buyers and in dollars for buyers abroad. The advice fits a clinic visit here: take your last three or four period dates, because a clinician finds them more useful than one calculator result.

✓A plan for the wait

The Trying-to-Conceive Blueprint is one payment of ₦5,700 early-bird (₦13,700 standard), or $12 early-bird ($27 standard) abroad. It walks you through your cycle, preparing, your partner, your tests and the wait. You also get lifetime access to a protected Conception community and an assistant that teaches and sends you to a doctor for anything clinical.

✓Clinic questions to print, and his own checklist

Fertility Appointment Questions lets you tick, add and print. His Fertility Checklist is for your partner to tick himself. Fertility involves both of you.

Where Flo is ahead

A bigger community

Flo's Secret Chats is a moderated chat space inside the app, and Flo says 7 million members chat there each month. Our Conception community is limited to Blueprint buyers.

Daily logging

Flo records symptoms, sends reminders and syncs across your devices. Our tools record period start dates and nothing else. If you want a log you add to every day, Flo does that better.

A bigger library and a longer history

Flo has a very large content library and about 70 million monthly users by its own count. If you already have years of logs there, that history stays with Flo, and starting here means starting from zero.

How the Wholesome Girlies calculator works

The Ovulation Calculator uses two numbers: the first day of your last period and your usual cycle length. It adds the cycle length to that date to find your next period, then counts back 14 days to estimate the day you ovulate. The fertile window it shows starts five days before that day and ends one day after it.

On a 28-day cycle, day 1 is the first day of your period. Your next period is due on day 29, ovulation is estimated on day 15, and the window runs from day 10 to day 16. That is the whole sum.

A calendar estimates and does not detect. A Journal of Medical Internet Research analysis of 7,043 tracked cycles found that ovulation does not sit reliably at the midpoint of a cycle, and the National Health Service says it usually happens 10 to 16 days before your next period. That limit applies to any calendar method, so we do not claim ours predicts better than Flo's.

Questions women ask

What does Flo cost in Nigeria?
On Android in Nigeria, Flo's Pass It On Project gives Premium at no charge for one year from the upgrade date, and Google Play's Nigerian listing says Premium costs nothing in your country. On iPhone, the Nigerian App Store sells Premium purchases from ₦3,900 (about $3) to ₦29,900 (about $22).
Does the Wholesome Girlies calculator predict better than Flo?
We do not claim that. It sets ovulation 14 days before your next period and the window from five days before to one day after. A Journal of Medical Internet Research analysis of 7,043 tracked cycles found ovulation does not sit reliably at the midpoint. A calendar predicts. It does not detect. Take your last three or four period dates to a clinician.
Where do my dates go?
They stay on your phone. The tools say: Saved only on this phone. Nothing is sent to us. A forget button clears them, and a backup file moves them to a new phone.
Can I use both?
Yes. Log in Flo if you like its symptoms and reminders, and use the Wholesome Girlies tools and the Blueprint for the plan, the clinic questions and the checklist for your partner.
Is this medical advice?
No. It is educational, not medical advice. See a doctor if you have been trying for a year, or six months if you are over 35.

When to see a doctor

See a doctor after twelve months of trying, or six months if you are over 35. See one sooner if your cycles are very irregular, shorter than 21 days or longer than 35, or have stopped. Take your last three or four period dates with you.
