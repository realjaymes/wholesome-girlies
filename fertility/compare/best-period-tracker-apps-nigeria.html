Best period and ovulation tracker apps in Nigeria

Last updated: October 2026

Written by Cynthia Obinatu, community and editorial lead · Every claim comes from a cited source. Not yet reviewed by a clinician, so read it with our medical policy.

Wholesome Girlies, Flo, Clue and Period Calendar, in our order for a woman trying to conceive

Our order is Wholesome Girlies, Flo, Period Calendar, then Clue. We ranked on what a woman trying to conceive in Nigeria needs: no account, dates kept on her phone, tools for the clinic, content that fits Nigeria, and how much daily tracking the app does. We did not rank on prediction accuracy, because no app on this page can detect ovulation from dates alone. Flo ranks 1 and Period Calendar ranks 2 in the Nigerian App Store Health and Fitness chart, and both send reminders.

We make Wholesome Girlies. Every claim about it comes from our own tools and prices, and every claim about another app comes from that app's own pages and its Nigerian store listing.

This page compares apps. It cannot tell you when you will conceive. A calendar predicts and does not detect. It is educational, not medical advice, and no clinician has reviewed it yet.

1. Wholesome Girlies

The Period Tracker and Ovulation Calculator open on the date field with no account and no card. Your dates stay on your phone, and after three logged periods the tracker uses your own average. The Trying-to-Conceive Blueprint, the appointment questions and the checklist for your partner sit beside them.

Best for: A woman trying to conceive who wants no account and a plan.

Watch for: No symptom logging and no reminders. The calculator is plain arithmetic, so we make no accuracy claim.

2. Flo

Flo is a full tracker. It logs symptoms, sends reminders and syncs across your devices, and it has a very large content library. It ranks 1 in the Nigerian App Store Health and Fitness chart, and Premium costs nothing on Android in Nigeria for one year under its Pass It On Project.

Best for: A woman who wants daily symptom logs, reminders and sync.

Watch for: Premium purchases on iPhone are between ₦3,900 (about $3) and ₦29,900 (about $22). Flo is written for a global audience.

3. Period Calendar

Period Calendar ranks 2 in the Nigerian App Store Health and Fitness chart and first in Nigerian Google Play search for period tracker. Nigerian reviewers praise its notifications and how long they have used it.

Best for: A woman who wants reminders and a calendar with years of history.

Watch for: Google Play marks it as containing ads, its Pro tier costs ₦69,900 (about $52), and reviews mention ads.

Questions women ask

Which period tracker app is best in Nigeria?
It depends on the job. Flo and Period Calendar rank 1 and 2 in the Nigerian App Store chart and track daily. Wholesome Girlies suits a woman trying to conceive who wants no account, dates on her phone and a plan for the clinic.
What does Flo cost in Nigeria?
On Android in Nigeria, Flo's Pass It On Project gives Premium at no charge for one year from the upgrade date, and Google Play's Nigerian listing says Premium costs nothing in your country. On iPhone, the Nigerian App Store sells Premium purchases from ₦3,900 (about $3) to ₦29,900 (about $22).
Which app predicts ovulation most accurately?
No app on this page can detect ovulation from dates alone, and we make no accuracy claim. A Journal of Medical Internet Research analysis of 7,043 tracked cycles found ovulation does not sit reliably at the midpoint. Take your last three or four period dates to a clinician.
Is this medical advice?
No. It is educational, not medical advice. See a doctor if you have been trying for a year, or six months if you are over 35.

When to see a doctor

See a doctor after twelve months of trying, or six months if you are over 35. See one sooner if your cycles are very irregular, shorter than 21 days or longer than 35, or have stopped. Take your last three or four period dates with you.
