# Wholesome Girlies site: build rules

Static HTML on GitHub Pages, no framework. Preview with `python3 serve.py 8001` from the repo root (clean URLs work as on GitHub Pages). Before browser-testing, check which folder owns the port (`lsof -nP -iTCP:8001`). Stub `window.fetch` before clicking any checkout path, so a test never writes a row to the Checkout Intents sheet.

These rules apply to every page. `scripts/check-pages.py` enforces the machine-checkable ones and runs before every deploy. Run it before you commit:

```
python3 scripts/check-pages.py
```

**Hard rules** hold on every page today, and any violation fails. **Ratchet rules** have a known backlog that is frozen per page in `scripts/check-baseline.json`. A page fails only if it gets worse, or a new page breaks the rule. When you fix backlog pages, run `python3 scripts/check-pages.py --update-baseline` to lock in the lower counts. Never raise a baseline count to make a check pass.

The full plans live in the Obsidian vault under `Areas/Work/Wholesome Girlies/`: page templates in `01 - Website Architecture & Build Plan.md`, analytics in `Analytics Setup.md`, consent in `Consent Banner Build Scope.md`, checkout in `Checkout Intent Setup.md`, SEO in `SEO/02 - SEO Implementation Plan.md`, compliance in `_Resources/Compliance - Green and Red Tiering.md`, copy in `Content/_ChatGPT Project — WG Copy Engine/`, and sharing, print, games, and the app in `06 - Virality Plan.md` (section 8). When a rule changes, update it here, in the check script, and in the vault note that owns it.

## 1. Analytics and tracking

- GTM container `GTM-KW443M88` loads in `<head>` on every page, with its noscript iframe straight after `<body>`. GTM is the only tag loader: no gtag.js, no pixel init, no on-site InitiateCheckout or Purchase (Selar fires both). Lead fires only from `checkout-intent.js`; ViewContent only from GTM. Never run one event in two layers. *(hard)*
- Any page that loads `checkout-intent.js` starts `<head>` with the identity clean-up script (`window.__idq`), which strips personal details from the URL before any tag reads it. *(hard)*
- Selar links use `selar.com/<slug>?add_to_cart=1&email=&fullname=&mobile=` with the six product slugs. Diaspora pages add `currency=USD`; Nigerian pages never do. The `MOTHER` coupon (35% off the bundle) is for Trying-to-Conceive and First Pregnancy Plan buyers only. Their program home's bundle card names the code and links to the bundle page with `?coupon=MOTHER`. On the bundle pages, `checkout-intent.js` adds `&coupon=MOTHER` to every buy button (Selar applies it at checkout) and shows her price on the buttons, the sticky bar and under the price card when that page came from the card or her home's flag is on the device. A bundle owner, a postpartum or First Baby buyer and every other visitor see the plain price. *(hard, except the coupon placement)*
- Share links carry `?ref=wa|x|fb|th|li|ig|link`, and clicks push `share_click` with `share_channel`, `share_item`, and `share_surface`.
- GTM tags that wait for consent fire "Unlimited", never "Once per page" (container setting). GTM changes are staged by the service account and published by James. QA in a clean incognito window.

## 2. Consent and privacy

- `consent-defaults.js` loads in `<head>` after the identity script and before GTM. Every page loads `cookieconsent.css`, `wg-consent.css`, `cookieconsent.umd.js`, and `wg-consent.js`, and its footer carries the "Privacy choices" link (`data-cc="show-preferencesModal"`). *(hard)*
- Browser storage keys start with `wg_`, so the consent guard covers them. Tools compute on her device and store the segment, never raw health inputs on a server. *(hard)*
- A shared link never carries her answers, dates, or results.

## 3. SEO

- Every indexable page has a canonical equal to its own clean URL (no `.html`), the full Open Graph and Twitter card set with `og:url` equal to the canonical and an `og:image` that exists, the three favicon links, exactly one `<h1>`, alt text on every image, and `rel="noopener"` on new-tab links. *(hard)*
- `/go/` bridges, thank-you hubs, diaspora sales pages, and `404` carry `noindex, nofollow` and stay out of `sitemap.xml`. `robots.txt` has no Disallow lines, because a Disallow stops Google reading the noindex. *(hard)*
- `sitemap.xml` lists every indexable page by clean URL with a `<lastmod>`, and only `wg_release.py` (in `~/Documents/site-qa-tools/`) edits it, on release day. *(hard)*
- JSON-LD parses on every page. Guides: Article or MedicalWebPage, plus FAQPage and BreadcrumbList. Tools: SoftwareApplication and BreadcrumbList, and FAQPage *(ratchet)*. Nigerian sales pages: Product, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList. Home: Organization and WebSite. *(hard)*
- Every guide and tool is carded on its stage index, and every tool on `/tools/` too. Nothing ships unlinked. Every internal link and asset resolves. *(hard)*
- Every guide that links a tool is linked back from that tool, and every tool links at least one guide in its stage and its stage hub. *(hard)* The check fails a tool that misses a link back, and a tool with no guide unless it is on the short, commented `NO_GUIDE` list in `scripts/check-pages.py`. Sensitive pages (loss, the mental-health check-ins, The Wait Check-in, warning signs) link only to care and their related guides.
- Meta descriptions run 155 characters or fewer *(ratchet)*; titles 50 to 60 characters, leading with the searched phrase.
- Tool trust layer: a Person author and a citation array in schema *(ratchet)*. Health pages that are sourced cite at least three recognised authorities and say plainly they are not yet reviewed by a clinician.
- **Every tool and guide has its own link preview card** at `/assets/img/og/<slug>.jpg?v=<version>`, used for both `og:image` and `twitter:image`, made by `scripts/make-og.js` *(hard)*. **Look A (sticker) for tools, games and quizzes**: the stage-tint page, the title, a "Try it" pill, a live screenshot of the tool on an ink-outlined sticker card (checklists ticked, calculators filled with a sample date) and the stage guide beside it; cast-free tools (The Wait Check-in, the Mind Check-in) sit on cream with no guide. Guide cards show a group chat: the hook from the Share Copy Bank winner (`scripts/og-hooks.json`, from `python3 scripts/share-bank.py --og-json`) as the message, the guide as the shared link; sensitive guides get a single heart reaction. **Every other indexable page has its own card too** (`PAGE_CARDS` in `scripts/og-looks.js`, run `make-og.js --pages`): the home, the stage hubs, `/tools/`, `/app/`, `/programs/`, the About pages and each program sales page alternate look A and look C (the carousel cover: very large title, speech bubble, big guide) page by page, and a diaspora sales page, its Nigerian page and both `/go/` bridges share one image. Only the legal pages, the 404 and the offline page use `/assets/img/og/default.jpg?v=<version>`, which is look C with the four stage guides. The Organization logo in schema is `icon-512.png`, never a card. Each tool run can also save a 1080×1350 (4:5) feed post of every card to `~/Downloads/Wholesome Girlies/Social Posts/<stage>/` (old tool and guide layouts, `--no-square` skips them; `--square-only` remakes just the posts). Result cards are look B (next bullet). When a card changes, re-render it and bump `OG_VERSION` in `make-og.js` and every meta tag that points at it, so WhatsApp, X and Facebook fetch the new image. When the brand look changes (the sticker and cream looks, the cast and stage guides, the wordmark, type or palette), the tool, guide, page and default cards and every result card are regenerated in the same session: design options render first with `--look a|b|c|all` into `_lab/og-preview/` (never over the real files), James picks, and only then do `make-og.js`, `make-og.js --pages` and `make-result-cards.js` run for real with the versions bumped.
- **Result cards** *(hard: the check fails a missing link preview or Status card, a missing result page, or a tool without `wgShowResult` and `wg-result-share`)*: every quiz or checker result type is listed in `scripts/results.json` with a cast scene in `assets/img/cast/results/`. `scripts/make-result-cards.js` renders its 1200×630 link preview and 1080×1920 Status card in look B (cream page, tint circle, ink-outlined sticker frame; `scripts/og-looks.js`) into `assets/img/results/<tool>/`, and `scripts/make-result-pages.py` writes the noindex result page at `/<stage>/tools/<tool>/result/<type>` plus `assets/js/wg-result.js`. The tool, or the game engine it loads (`wg-pair.js`, `wg-chatcard.js`, `wg-tierlist.js`), calls `wgShowResult(type)` after scoring, and the page carries `<div id="wg-result-share" hidden>` in its result panel. A result link never carries her answers. Results that show a safety route (control, threats, force) never get a share row. Words go in the layout, never in the AI art.
- **Brand profiles are the same five everywhere** *(hard)*: Facebook `/wholesomegirlies`, Instagram `@wholesomegirlieshq`, TikTok `@wholesomegirlies`, X `@wgirlieshq`, LinkedIn `/company/wholesomegirlies`. The registry is the vault's `05 - Accounts & Platform IDs.md`.

## 6. Compliance

- Green tier only. Every health tool and guide carries "educational, not medical advice" and a when-to-see-a-doctor or safety block *(ratchet)*. A game or quiz tagged Game or Quiz on `/tools/` is exempt unless it states health facts (`HEALTH_GAMES` in `scripts/check-pages.py`: both Old Wives' Tales, Milestone Guess and Visitors Bingo, which also say plainly they are not yet reviewed by a clinician). A non-health game carries a one-line "this is for fun" note instead, and no doctor box. Mental-health content routes to real care and MANI on 0809 111 6264.
- No review claim ("medically reviewed by", "reviewed by professionals") until a named clinician has signed. The credibility ceiling is "research and data-backed", or "sourced, not yet reviewed by a clinician". *(hard)*
- Never imply we know her condition. No diagnose, treat, or cure claims. No outcome or timeline promises: no conception promise, no marriage guarantee, no birth or body promise. *(judgement)*
- Banned trigger phrases: "get pregnant fast", "boost fertility", "bounce back", "get your body back", "sleep through the night", "make him marry you", "make him choose you". Guides may quote a searched question in order to answer it; share lines and every other page may not. *(hard)*
- Honest urgency only: no countdowns, fake scarcity, or guaranteed results. *(hard)* Testimonials are verbatim from the proof file with first name and initial, stage, and city. Sales pages with testimonials carry a results-vary line *(ratchet)*. Only the 6-Week Postpartum Reset ("300+ first-time mums") and the Wife Material Blueprint ("300+ women") may cite a number.

Wherever games and other tools share a list, the games come first: the homepage Tools grid *(hard: the check fails a game card after a tool card)*, the tool reels on sales pages *(hard: `scripts/build-tool-reels.py` sorts games first, then the program's order list, and the check fails a stale reel)*, and the app's games block (section 10).

The pipeline: ship the tool, write its brief, render the short, run `node scripts/publish-to-site.mjs <tool-slug>` in `~/Documents/site-motion-kit` (cuts the loop and poster into `assets/video/tool-shorts/` and `assets/img/tool-shorts/`, writes the tool's entry in `assets/data/tool-shorts.json` with a fresh `tool_fingerprint`, then runs `scripts/build-tool-reels.py`), and the reels update on every page the entry lists. ## 10. The app (website and app ship together)

The installed app is the website. "Girlies" on her home screen opens `/app/` from `manifest.webmanifest`, and `sw.js` saves pages and files so the tools work without data. There is no separate app build and no store: one push to `main` ships the website and the app together. These rules keep the two from drifting apart.

- **How an update reaches her.** Pages load from the network first, so a pushed page shows in the app the next time she opens it online. Offline she sees the copy from her last visit. Files in `/assets/` load from the cache first, so a changed asset reaches the app only through its `?v=` bump (section 9). An asset changed without a bump stays old in the app indefinitely. *(hard: mixed versions fail)*
- **Every page carries the app tags** *(hard)*: the manifest link, `theme-color`, `apple-mobile-web-app-title` ("Girlies"), `wg-app.js`, and "Get the app" in the footer Explore list (never in the menu). After adding pages, run `python3 scripts/add-app-tags.py`, then `python3 scripts/build-tool-reels.py`, which keeps the reel script last before `</body>`. When `wg-app.js` changes, bump `APP_JS_VERSION` in `add-app-tags.py` and run it again.
- **Copy reads right in the app and on the website** *(hard)*. One page serves both, and a reader in the installed app has no site and no browser. Visible text says "here", "on Wholesome Girlies" or "on this device", never "site", "website" or "browser". Where the word is needed, wrap it as `<span data-app-word="app">site</span>` and `appWords()` in `wg-app.js` swaps it inside the installed app (JS that writes visible text reads `WGApp.standalone` instead). `scripts/check-pages.py` fails any such word in body text outside a `data-app-word` span; it skips scripts, JSON-LD, comments, attributes, URLs, `<head>` and meta tags (only seen outside the app) and `/legal/` (where "website" is the legal term), and `ALLOW_WEB_WORDS` holds the few lines that name another website, each with its reason. Generators (`make-result-pages.py`) follow the same rule.
- **`sw.js`.** Bump `VERSION` whenever `sw.js` changes (its logic, `CORE`, `SKIP` or `LATER`). The new worker takes over on her next open and deletes the old cache. Ad bridges, sales pages and video clips never get cached. Reel posters are cached when she scrolls to them, never on install, so installing costs little data.
- **A new tool** works offline only if `/tools/` links it, because the worker saves every tool linked from `/tools/` on install (section 3 already requires the link). If it saves entries, add its storage key to `APP_TOOLS` in `app/index.html` so "Your saved tools" shows it *(hard)*. A tool that only reads another tool's entries and saves none of its own (Mum Wrapped reads the Feeding and Sleep Tracker) goes on `READS_ONLY` in `scripts/check-pages.py` instead. If it is sensitive (loss, mental health, warning signs), add its path to `QUIET` in `wg-app.js` so no install offer appears on it. Never rename a storage key without moving her old entries across, because the site and the app lose them together.
- **The app's two games are James's pick**: Are You a Girl's Girl? then Is He Husband Material?, under "Games to play and send to a friend" on `/app/`. A new game does not replace them; change the pair only when James picks others, and update `APP_TOP_GAMES` in `scripts/check-pages.py` in the same push. *(hard: the check fails any other pair or order, and any card that is not a tool tagged Game or Quiz on `/tools/`.)* The app home carries no tool videos *(hard: the check fails a reel on `/app/`)*.
- **The random game button** ("Spin for a game", `assets/js/wg-random-game.js`, James's pick) sits above the search on `/tools/` and under the greeting on `/app/`. It spins through the game names, stops on one card with its line and "Play it", and becomes "Spin again", so she chooses; it never opens a game on its own and never repeats the last game it gave her. Its pool is `assets/data/games.json`, which `scripts/build-game-pool.py` writes from the Game and Quiz cards on `/tools/`, so rerun it whenever a game is carded there.
- **The manifest** (name, icons, colours, `start_url`) changes only with James's go. Each program home and its reader point at the program's own copy, `manifests/<slug>.webmanifest`, which `scripts/build-program-homes.py` writes: identical to the main manifest (same `id`, so it is the same app) except `start_url` is `/app/?home=<slug>`. Adding the app from a program home therefore opens `/app/` with the program already saved. `sw.js` does not cache these files; the browser reads them when she installs. *(hard: the check fails a missing or stale one)* Android Chrome checks for a changed manifest when she opens the app, at most once a day. iPhone keeps the old name and icon until she adds the app again.
- **The install offers.** A card ("Get the Girlies app" on a phone, "Get Girlies on your phone" on a computer) sits under game and tool results, on thank-you hubs and on the homepage after the programs block (its section stays hidden when no card is offered). A bottom bar appears on phones after she saves a tool entry, at most once a visit and 14 days quiet after she closes it. Neither appears inside the installed app, on sales pages, on `/go/` bridges or on the `QUIET` pages. The `app_*` events go to the dataLayer without her entries. Tag Manager tags for them are staged by the service account and published by James.
- **The steps sheet matches her browser**, because only Android Chrome has an install button a page can trigger. iPhone Safari gets the iOS 26 steps (••• → Share → View More → Add to Home Screen) with the recording. iPhone Chrome gets Share at the top right, and Edge and Firefox get their menu. In-app browsers (Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp) are told to open the page in their browser first. Android gets the install prompt or the menu steps, and a computer gets a QR code that opens `/app/` on her phone. Check every variant from a Mac by adding `?preview=iphone`, `iphone-chrome`, `android`, `in-app`, `computer` or `installed` (the app as it opens from an iPhone home screen) to any page. When Apple or Google move the menu items, rewrite these steps and re-record the clip.
