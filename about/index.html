About us

Women-led, and built on research and real data

Wholesome Girlies is a community of women making the health, confidence, and relationship side of life a little clearer, at every stage, for African women at home and in the diaspora.

Why we exist

Women carry a set of complications the world still treats as an afterthought, for the simple fact that they have reproductive systems. Getting real, specialized care for any of it is hard, especially in Nigeria, where there is a genuine shortage of obstetricians, gynaecologists, and even basic healthcare.

So when a woman goes looking for real answers about her body, the internet mostly hands her noise, fear, and things to buy. We wanted the opposite. Every woman deserves immediate support, expert advice, and a community that truly understands: simple tools that solve a real problem in a minute, honest guidance in plain language, and women who have been where she is.

We start with the journey into motherhood, fertility, pregnancy, postpartum, and parenting, because it is one of the most searched and least supported stretches of a woman's life. Over time we grow into the rest: wellness, beauty and confidence, hormones, and relationships.

How we keep it honest

Everything health-related is built on research and real data, and every tool tells you plainly when something needs a real doctor. We will never pretend to be one. We would rather send you to care than keep you on a page. As licensed professionals join us to review our health content and run our workshops, you will see them named here.

Who is behind it

Wholesome Girlies is women-led, and we are building it with a growing circle of women, from creators to the clinicians who review our health content. It is a collective, not a personality. As more real women come on board, you will meet them here.
