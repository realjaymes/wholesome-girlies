About us

Built by women, backed by research and real data

Wholesome Girlies is a community of women making the health, beauty, confidence, and relationship side of life a little clearer, at every stage.

Why we exist

When women look for real answers about their bodies and their lives, the internet gives them noise, fear, and things to buy. We wanted the opposite: simple tools that solve a real problem in a minute, honest guidance in plain language, and a community that has been where you are.

We start with the journey into motherhood, fertility, pregnancy, postpartum, and parenting, because it is one of the most searched, least supported stretches of a woman's life. Over time we grow into the rest: weight and wellness, beauty and confidence, hormones, and relationships.

How we keep it honest

Anything health-related is built on research and real data, and every tool tells you plainly when something needs a real doctor. We will never pretend to be one. We would rather send you to care than keep you on a page.

Who is behind it

We are a small team of women and allies. Our brand and community are led by women, and our health content is built on research and real data. As we grow, we are partnering with more women, from creators to clinicians, to make this genuinely theirs.
