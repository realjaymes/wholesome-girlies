Finish Mummy's Sentence

Mummy starts the line. You finish it. One new Nigerian mum line every day, four tries, and a row of squares to send to the group chat.

This is a game for fun. The lines are the kind Nigerian mums say with love, and they are meant to make you laugh. Your streak and your tries stay on your phone. Nothing you play is sent anywhere.

When to see a doctor

This page is a game and gives no health advice. If you are worried about your baby, or about how you are feeling, speak to a doctor or nurse.

Written by Cynthia Obinatu, community and editorial lead

How to play

You see the start of a line Nigerian mums say, and one word is missing. Guess the word in four tries.

  1. Read today's line.
  2. Type the missing word, or tap Pick from four. Typing shows green for a letter in the right place and yellow for a letter in the word.
  3. Use the clue if you need it.
  4. Copy your squares and send them to the group chat. Your squares never show the answer.

The same sentence for everyone

The date picks the line, so there is no sign-up and no waiting on a server. Your sister in Houston and your friend in Enugu get the same sentence on the same day. A new one arrives at midnight on your phone.

New to being a mum yourself? You are about to start saying some of these lines. Starting solids is a good first guide for the baby years.

Common questions

Is everyone's sentence the same?
Yes. The date picks the sentence, so every woman who plays on the same day gets the same line. Compare your squares with your sister or your group chat.
What do the squares mean?
One square for each try. Green means you found the word. Yellow means you were close. White means that guess missed. Your squares never show the answer.
Can I pick from options instead of typing?
Yes. Tap Pick from four to choose from four words. Typing shows you which letters are right and in the right place, like Wordle.
Does it save my streak?
Yes, on your phone only. It counts the days in a row that you finish the sentence, found or not. Nothing is sent to us.
Are the lines in pidgin too?
Some are. Those lines carry a Pidgin tag.
Why do the lines repeat?
The game cycles through its whole list of lines and then starts again. We add new lines to the end.
