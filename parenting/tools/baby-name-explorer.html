Baby Name Explorer

Search Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa baby names by meaning, or filter by girl, boy and origin. Arabic, Hebrew, English and other African names are in the list too. When you have chosen the name, make a naming card with its meaning to share after the naming ceremony. If your name is not on the list, add it yourself.

Can't find your name? Add it yourself.

Type the name first.

The name stays on this page, on your device. We never see it or save it.

Your naming card

The card and the link show only the name, its meaning and where it comes from. They never show a birth date, weight, place of birth or photo.
