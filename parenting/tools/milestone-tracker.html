Baby Milestone Tracker

Choose your baby's age to see the milestones many babies reach around then. Every baby is different and reaches these at their own pace, this is a gentle guide, never a test.

Educational, not medical advice. Milestones are typical ranges, not deadlines. Babies develop at very different rates, and reaching something later is often completely normal.

When to speak to a doctor

Mention it at a check-up if your baby is not meeting several milestones for their age, seems to lose a skill they had, does not respond to sounds or faces, or if your instinct says something is different. Early support helps, and asking is never an overreaction.
