Baby Routine Planner

Choose your baby's age for a gentle sample rhythm of feeds, naps, and play. Babies are not clocks, so treat this as a soft guide you can shape around your own baby, never a rule.

Educational, not medical advice. This is a sample rhythm, not a schedule your baby must follow. Feed on demand as your baby needs, especially in the early months, and follow your baby's cues over any clock.
