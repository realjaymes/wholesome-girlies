Baby Feeding & Sleep Tracker

Tap to log a feed or a sleep as it happens. Over a day or two you will start to see your baby's rhythm, and you will have a clean summary to share with your doctor. Everything saves on this device only.

Today

    No entries yet today. Tap a button above the moment a feed or sleep happens.

    Educational, not medical advice. This tracker helps you notice patterns. It does not tell you whether your baby is feeding or sleeping enough, only your doctor or nurse can judge that. It saves only on your own device.

    When to see a doctor

    Speak to a health professional if your baby is feeding much less than usual, is hard to wake for feeds, has far fewer wet nappies, is not gaining weight, or you are worried in any way. Trust your instinct, and get help early.
