# Clue fact sheet

**Feeds:** H04 and later Clue pages.

**Provenance:** Every row below was read from Clue's official support, privacy policy, and Apple App Store listings on 26 November 2026.

## Price by market

| Market | Fact | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nigeria | The App Store lists Clue Plus purchases from ₦1,500 to ₦12,900. | https://apps.apple.com/ng/app/clue-period-cycle-tracker/id657189652 |
| United Kingdom | The App Store lists £7.99 for one month and yearly purchases up to £24.99. | https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/clue-period-cycle-tracker/id657189652 |
| United States | The App Store lists $9.99 for one month and $39.99 for one year. | https://apps.apple.com/us/app/clue-period-cycle-tracker/id657189652 |

Clue says prices vary by store region and appear on the subscription screen: https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005215266-How-much-does-Clue-Plus-cost

## Features and availability

The Nigerian App Store lists cycle and period predictions, more than 200 symptoms and experiences, reminders, pregnancy tracking, Clue Conceive, and integrations with Apple Watch, Oura, WHOOP, Withings and Fitbit. It lists BioWink GmbH in Berlin as the provider. The app is available in Nigeria, the United Kingdom and the United States.

## Data practice, quoted exactly

Clue's privacy policy says: "the sensitive health data you track in the Clue app is never shared with or sold to advertisers." It also says Clue shares a minimal amount of technical data with advertising networks and that this can be turned off.

Source: https://helloclue.com/privacy
