Baby Name Battle

Twelve pairs of Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa and other names. Each of you picks one name from every pair. Then you see the names you both chose and the ones to argue about.

This is a game for choosing names together. It is for fun and conversation. It does not say anything about your baby or your pregnancy. Name meanings can differ by family and spelling. Educational, not medical advice, and not yet reviewed by a clinician.

When to see a doctor now

This game is about names and fun, not your health. Any bleeding, or a change in your baby's movements, means you contact your doctor or maternity unit straight away. Also go in for severe pain or a fall or blow to your bump.

Written by Cynthia Obinatu, community and editorial lead

How Baby Name Battle works

You choose which names to pick from: girl names, boy names or all names. You then see twelve pairs and pick one name from each. When you finish, you get a link to send to your partner on WhatsApp. They pick from the same twelve pairs, and then you both see the result.

  1. Choose the names you want to pick from.
  2. Pick one name from each of the twelve pairs.
  3. Send the link to your partner.
  4. They pick, and you both see the names you chose and the names to argue about.

What to do with your result

The names you both chose are a good place to start your shortlist. The names to argue about are worth a proper talk. Read the meaning of each one aloud, and say why it matters to you: a grandparent, a language, a sound you like. Naming can carry family history, so give it time.

Want to look up other names? The Baby Name Explorer lists meanings and origins.

What the link carries

The link carries your 12 picks (one tap each), the name set you chose, and your first name if you type one. It carries nothing else: no dates, no health details and no results from other tools. It sits after the # in the address, so it is never sent to our server or to analytics, and we store nothing. Anyone you give the link to can read your picks.

Common questions

Does this tell me what my baby will be?
No. The names are only a list to choose from. Pick the set you like. Nothing in this game predicts or says anything about your baby.
What if we choose different names every time?
Then you have a good list to talk through. Read the meanings together and ask what each name means to the other person. You can also start again with a different name set.
Where do the meanings come from?
The names and meanings are the same list as the Baby Name Explorer. Many Nigerian names have more than one meaning and more than one spelling, so ask an elder from your family before you decide.
Is my partner's phone number or name saved?
No. We store nothing. The link carries your picks and your first name only if you type it.
