Hospital Bag Checklist

Everything to pack for labour, birth, and the first day or two, for you, your baby, and whoever is with you. Pack it from about 36 weeks. Your progress saves on this device.

Educational, not medical advice. This is a general packing list. Your hospital or midwife may have their own list, always follow that too. It saves only on your own device.

When to go in

Contact your hospital or midwife if your waters break, if contractions are strong and regular, if you have bleeding, if the baby is moving less, or if you are simply unsure. It is always fine to call.
