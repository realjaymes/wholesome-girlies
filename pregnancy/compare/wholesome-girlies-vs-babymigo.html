Wholesome Girlies vs Babymigo

Last updated: November 2026

Written by Cynthia Obinatu, community and editorial lead · Every claim comes from a cited source. Not yet reviewed by a clinician, so read it with our medical policy.

For women preparing for their first pregnancy in Nigeria

If you want to find a hospital or a lactation consultant near you, ask other mothers and doctors a question, or join a live online antenatal class, use Babymigo. If you want a week-by-week plan for your first pregnancy, use Wholesome Girlies. Our First Pregnancy Plan explains what is normal each week, what you can eat, what your scans mean and when to call the doctor. You can also print a list of questions to take to each antenatal visit. You can use both, and we do not claim our advice is better than Babymigo's.

Wholesome Girlies is ours, so we are not neutral. We use our own tools and prices for facts about it. We use each app's own pages and Nigerian App Store or Google Play listing for facts about that app.

This page compares a parenting forum and a plan. It cannot tell you what is normal for your pregnancy. It is educational, not medical advice, and no clinician has reviewed it yet.

Where Wholesome Girlies is ahead

✓You know what to do each week

The First Pregnancy Plan takes you from your first weeks to the first days at home with your baby. It tells you which symptoms are common, which need a call and which mean you go in. It explains your scans and tests, covers labour, the hospital bag and your birth plan, and you join a moderated community of women at your stage for life. Put it to use, and if it is still not right for you, you can ask for a full refund within 30 days.

✓One payment

The Plan is ₦5,700 early-bird (₦13,700 standard), paid once, or $12 early-bird ($27 standard) abroad. There is no subscription and nothing to cancel.

✓Food advice for what you actually eat

The Plan covers jollof, suya, local fish, zobo and herbal mixtures. The Safe Foods Checklist covers moi moi, ugu and beans, and our guide answers whether tiger nut, bitter kola and cloves are safe in pregnancy.

✓The tools need no account and no card

The pregnancy tools open with no sign-in and no payment step. The Birth Plan Builder makes a one-page birth plan for your midwife or doctor. The Hospital Bag Checklist covers your hospital bag. The Newborn Essentials Checklist lists baby essentials. The Plan opens with the email you paid with.

✓Your dates stay on your phone

Your date stays on your phone when you use the due date calculator or weekly tracker. We do not receive it.

✓Questions to print for the clinic

Antenatal Appointment Questions lets you tick, add your own and print.

✓A new phone, and no data needed

Save a backup puts your saved entries in one file. Restore a backup moves that file to a new phone. Email sign-in moves your programs and streak, the count of days you use Wholesome Girlies in a row. It never moves your dates.

✓Something short for your pregnancy week

Today's card is a one or two sentence note on the Girlies app home for your pregnancy week. It links to the tool or guide behind it. Under your due date result, you can ask for a weekly pregnancy email.

✓It stays with you after the birth

Wholesome Girlies also covers the weeks after the birth and raising your child, so the same Girlies app keeps working for you once your baby arrives.

Questions women ask

What does PreggClass cost?
Babymigo's own page shows PreggClass for individuals, partners or couples at ₦10,000 (about $7.50) a class, beside ₦20,500 (about $15) struck through. The corporate plan shows no price. It is a class, and The First Pregnancy Plan is a plan, so the two are different products.
How many members does Babymigo have?
Its home page says "Join 250,000+ members". That is Babymigo's own count.
Does the Wholesome Girlies Plan need an account?
The tools need no account and no card. The Plan opens when you sign in with the email you paid with. We send a sign-in link to that email.
Can I use both?
Yes. Use Babymigo's directory and forum, and use the Wholesome Girlies tools and The First Pregnancy Plan for the week by week plan and the questions for your visit.
Is this medical advice?
No. It is educational, not medical advice. The Plan says when a symptom belongs with your doctor.

When to see a doctor

See a doctor or go to hospital if you have bleeding, severe or persistent pain, fever, vomiting so severe you cannot keep fluids down, a bad headache with blurred vision, sudden swelling of the face and hands, or fluid leaking. If you are unsure, call. Nobody minds a call that turns out to be nothing.
