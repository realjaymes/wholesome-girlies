Partner Support Planner

In the first weeks, it is hard to ask for help, and even harder to say exactly what would help. Tick what you need most right now, add anything in your own words, and get a clear little note you can send your partner or family. Your choices save on this device.

What would help you most right now?

Tick as many or as few as you like. There is no wrong answer, and it can change day to day.

    What I need from you right now

    Educational, not medical advice. This planner is a gentle way to put words to what you need and share it with the people around you. It is a communication aid, not a health check or an assessment of any kind. Your choices save only on your own device.
