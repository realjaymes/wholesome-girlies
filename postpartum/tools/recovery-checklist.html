Postpartum Recovery Checklist

A gentle checklist for your first six weeks: healing your body, feeding your baby, and looking after your mind. Tick things off as you go, your progress saves on this device.

0 of 0 done
    Educational, not medical advice. This checklist is a gentle guide for a typical recovery. It does not replace your postnatal appointments or your doctor's advice. It saves only on your own device.

    When to see a doctor

    Do not wait to tick a box if something feels wrong. Heavy bleeding, a fever, severe or worsening pain, a bad headache with vision changes, foul-smelling discharge, or dark thoughts all need a doctor now.
