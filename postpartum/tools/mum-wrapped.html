How Mum Wrapped works

Mum Wrapped turns the numbers from your first six weeks into a short set of cards, like a year-end music recap. Nothing in it compares you to anyone.

  1. Open the page on the phone where you used the Feeding and Sleep Tracker, or type rough numbers.
  2. Add any fun extras you remember, like visitors and cold cups of tea.
  3. Tap through your cards, one at a time.
  4. Save the last card for your Status, or share your mum title with a friend.

What it counts

From the Baby Feeding and Sleep Tracker it counts feeds, sleeps and diapers, the taps you made between 10pm and 5am, your busiest night, and the longest stretch between two feeds you logged. Nothing else leaves the tracker.

Newborns feed often, day and night, and most do not sleep for long stretches in the first weeks. Counting it shows how much work that is. If you logged very little, your cards still celebrate what you did log.

Common questions

Does Mum Wrapped keep my numbers?
No. It reads your Feeding and Sleep Tracker on your phone and works out the counts there. Nothing is sent to us. If you share your result, the link and the picture show your mum title only.
What if I did not use the tracker?
Type the rough numbers you remember. A feeds-per-day number is enough to make your cards, and the extras are only for fun.
What does my Status card show?
Counts only, like the number of feeds and diapers. It never shows a baby's name, a date, a birth detail or anything about your health.
Is Mum Wrapped a health check?
No. It is a keepsake. It cannot tell you whether your baby is feeding or sleeping enough, and it does not judge how your six weeks went.
