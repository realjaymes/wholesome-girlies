Postpartum Recovery Timeline Calculator

Enter your delivery date and how you delivered, and we will show you gentle, week-by-week milestones for what many women experience as they heal. Every body is different, this is a guide, not a rule.

Your gentle recovery timeline

Educational, not medical advice. This tool shows general patterns of postpartum recovery. It does not diagnose or treat anything, and your own recovery may look different. It runs entirely in your browser, we do not store your date.

When to see a doctor

Call your doctor or go to a clinic straight away if you have heavy bleeding that soaks a pad an hour, a fever, severe pain, a bad headache with blurred vision, foul-smelling discharge, or thoughts of harming yourself or your baby. These need real care, not a calculator.
