Are You Ready for Love? Quiz

Eight honest questions about what you want, your standards, your healing, and whether you are dating from calm or from pressure. There is no pass or fail, and no score for any man's approval. This is a mirror for your own clarity.

Where you are already strong

    Where a little clarity helps

      Wherever you landed, this is a starting point, not a verdict. You are allowed to be a work in progress and worthy at the same time.

      This is reflection, not a rating. The quiz turns a few honest questions into a picture of where you are right now. It is not a test you can fail, and it says nothing about whether you deserve love. It runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you answer is saved or sent anywhere.

      How to use this

      1. Answer each question honestly, for yourself, not for how you wish you felt.
      2. Tap Show my read to see your strengths and the one or two areas where more clarity would help.
      3. Sit with the "where a little clarity helps" list. That is your gentle to-do, not a flaw.
      4. Retake it in a few months. Readiness is something you build, not something you are born with or without.

      Questions women ask

      Does a low score mean something is wrong with me?

      No. A lower score usually means life has been heavy lately, or you have not had the space to get clear. It is information, not a judgement. Nobody is behind.

      Is this about becoming "wife material"?

      No. There is no checklist to pass to earn a man. This is about your own clarity and calm, so you choose well and are chosen well, as you already are.

      Should I stay single until I score high?

      Not necessarily. You can date while you grow. The point is to date from a grounded place instead of from fear or pressure, and this shows you where that grounding is strong.

      Is my data private?

      Completely. The quiz runs on your device only. We never see your answers, and nothing is stored after you close the tab.
