Couple Alignment

Money, children, family, where to live, faith at home and work after children. Each of you answers the same 12 questions. Then you see where you agree and what to talk about.

These questions start a conversation. They do not tell you whether to marry or stay with someone, and they do not replace counselling. Answer for what you want now. The game runs on your phone, and your answers travel only in the link you send.

If you are afraid of how your partner will react

If answering these questions makes you afraid, or a partner controls, threatens or hurts you, that matters more than any answer. If you are in immediate danger, call 112. For confidential help in Nigeria, reach out to WARIF, the Mirabel Centre, or the Lagos State DSVA; in the diaspora, your local domestic-abuse helpline.

Written by Cynthia Obinatu, community and editorial lead

How Couple Alignment works

You answer 12 questions across six topics: money, children, family boundaries, where to live, faith at home and work after children. When you finish, you get a link to send to your partner. They answer the same 12 questions, and then you both see each answer side by side.

  1. Answer the 12 questions for yourself.
  2. Send the link to your partner.
  3. They answer the same questions.
  4. Compare, and talk about the answers that differ.

What to do with your result

Each answer shows as “You agree” or “Talk about this”. Agreeing on everything is not the goal. Many couples differ on some of these, and the ones you differ on are the ones to talk through, ideally face to face. Ask what is behind the answer before you decide who is right. “Not sure yet” is a fair answer, and it also shows up as something to talk about.

These questions are a conversation starter. They do not tell you whether to marry, and they do not replace pre-marriage counselling from a faith leader or counsellor you trust.

What the link carries

The link carries your 12 answers (one tap each) and your first name if you type one. It carries nothing else: no dates, no health details and no results from other tools. It sits after the # in the address, so it is never sent to our server or to analytics, and we store nothing. Anyone you give the link to can read your answers, so send it to your partner only.

Common questions

Is there a right set of answers?
No. The game shows where you match and where you do not. The answers that differ are the ones worth a conversation.
Can we use this before we are engaged?
Yes. It works for couples before marriage and in the early years of marriage. Answer for what you want now, and come back to it as your lives change.
What if my partner and I do not share a faith?
The faith questions are written for every faith and for none. They ask how much practice you want at home and how children learn about it, so each of you can answer for yourself.
Is this private?
Yes. Your answers go from your phone to your partner's in the link. We do not store them and we cannot read them.
