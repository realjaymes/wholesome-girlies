Are You a Girl's Girl?

Your friend's ex asks you out and says nobody has to know. What you do next says plenty. Pick your answer in ten friendship situations and find out what kind of girl's girl you are.

1. Your friend's ex sends you a message. He says he has always liked you and asks you out. What do you do?
2. A group chat starts gossiping about your friend, but she is not in the chat. What do you do?
3. Your friend sends the aso ebi details for her owambe. The fabric and sewing will cost more than you planned. What do you do?
4. Your friend launches her small business after months of talking about it. What do you do?
5. Your friend calls at 1am. Her relationship has ended and she can barely get the words out. What do you do?
6. Your friend's man sends you a flirty DM, then says he was only joking. What do you do?
7. You and your friend applied for the same job. She gets it and you do not. What do you do?
8. Your friend asks you to tell her mum she is sleeping at your place, but she will be somewhere else. What do you do?
9. Your friend asks to borrow money again. She has not finished paying back the last one. What do you do?
10. Your friend is dating a man you do not like. She is happy, but you have seen him speak badly to her twice. What do you do?

What a girl's girl does in each one

    These are made-up situations. They are here for fun and conversation, and they say nothing about any friend you know. Real friendships have more context than four answers can hold. Your picks stay on your phone. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

    If a friend is not safe

    If a friend tells you a man controls her, threatens her or hurts her, believe her and stay close. If she is in immediate danger, call 112. For confidential help in Nigeria, reach out to WARIF, the Mirabel Centre, or the Lagos State DSVA; in the diaspora, her local domestic-abuse helpline.

    Written by Cynthia Obinatu, community and editorial lead

    How the Girl's Girl quiz works

    You will see ten friendship situations that Nigerian women know well, from aso ebi money to a 1am breakup call. Pick the answer closest to what you would do. Your choices reveal how you tend to show up for your friends.

    1. Read one situation at a time.
    2. Pick the answer closest to you.
    3. See your girl's girl type.
    4. Send your result to the group chat.

    What your result means

    Your result shows your usual friendship style. It is not a score for whether you are a good friend. You may be loud in one friendship, quiet in another and late when life gets busy. Use the result to notice how your care reaches the people you love. The pick-me result means your answers often put your own comfort, spotlight or a man's attention before your friend, and it gives you a place to start.

    A friend dating a man who speaks badly to her is one of the hardest situations here. If you are worried about a man in a friend's life, read red flags in a man before you talk to her, so you can name what you saw.

    Common questions

    Can I get more than one type?
    Your result shows the type that appears most in your answers. You may still recognise yourself in the other types.
    Is one result better than the others?
    Some patterns support friendship better than others, but the quiz is not ranking your worth. The pick-me result names a habit you can change, just as every result gives you something to notice and practise.
    What if none of the answers is exactly what I would do?
    Pick the closest one. Real friendships have more context than four answers can hold.
    Is this private?
    Yes. Your picks stay on your phone. If you share your result, the link and the picture show your result only, never your picks.
