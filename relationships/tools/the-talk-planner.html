The Talk Planner

The conversation about where things are going does not have to be a fight or a trap. It is just honesty. Use these prompts to get clear, then write your own notes. This is for you, and it saves on this device.

Sit with these gently. There are no right answers.

  • What do I actually want from this relationship?
  • What do I need to know about where he stands?
  • What have I been afraid to say out loud?
  • What will I do if his answer is not what I hope?
  • How do I want to feel walking away from this talk?

Keep it honest, and keep it kind

The aim is clarity, not winning and not trapping. Say what you want plainly, ask what you need to know, and listen to the real answer, including the one his actions give you. You are allowed to want commitment, and you are allowed to walk if it is not there. If a partner ever frightens, controls, or hurts you, please reach out to someone you trust and a domestic-violence helpline. Your safety comes before any relationship.

Guidance, not therapy or medical advice. This is support for your dating life. It is not counselling, legal, or medical advice. What you write is saved only on your own device.

Your safety first

This is about choosing well, never about tolerating harm. If a partner frightens you, controls you, isolates you, or hurts you, that is not something to manage or fix yourself. Please reach out to someone you trust and a domestic-violence helpline. You deserve to be safe.
